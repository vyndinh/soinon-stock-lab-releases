Install and verify

Soinon Stock Lab is a terminal application. On macOS, open Terminal and use the commands shown for your package; do not rely on double-clicking the vnt file in Finder.

  1. Choose the archive matching your operating system and CPU architecture.
  2. Verify its SHA-256 value against the checksum shown here or checksums.txt.
  3. Open “First-launch commands” under your download and copy the exact extraction and launch commands.
  4. Run version, then doctor, before starting tui.

Only download from this page or the official public Releases page. Do not use files attached to issues, comments, forks, or unofficial mirrors.

Current prerelease binaries are unsigned and not notarized. Your operating system may warn about or refuse the download. Do not disable operating-system security protections to run it; report a blocked normal launch through the support link below.

Local data and privacy

Product data is stored locally and telemetry is off by default. Public/no-key market and news feeds may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, or changed by their providers. Remove portfolio details, local paths, database contents, and private logs before sharing diagnostics.
